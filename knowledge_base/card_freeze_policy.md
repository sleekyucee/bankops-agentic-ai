# Card Freeze Policy

## Purpose
Use a card freeze when a customer reports suspicious card activity, loss of card control, or an urgent account access concern involving card payments.

## When to Freeze
- Customer confirms an unrecognized card transaction.
- Customer reports the card as lost, stolen, or compromised.
- Fraud monitoring identifies high-risk activity.
- Support cannot verify that the customer still controls the card.

## Support Action
Freeze the card immediately for high-risk reports. Confirm that the freeze blocks new card purchases, online card payments, and cash withdrawals. Existing pending transactions may still complete.

## Customer Message
Tell the customer their card has been frozen to prevent further card activity. Advise them to review recent transactions and confirm whether a replacement card is needed.

## Reversal
Only unfreeze a card after the customer confirms the activity is legitimate and account risk has been reviewed.
