# Fraud Dispute Process

## Purpose
Use the fraud dispute process when a customer reports a transaction they did not authorize.

## Intake Requirements
- Customer user ID or verified profile.
- Transaction merchant, amount, and date if available.
- Confirmation that the customer does not recognize or authorize the charge.
- Current card status and any active fraud ticket.

## Process
Create or update a fraud case, mark the risk level, and record the flagged transaction. If the card is active and risk is medium or high, recommend freezing the card while the case is reviewed.

## Customer Message
Explain that the transaction has been flagged for review, summarize the merchant and amount, and provide the next step clearly.

## Handoff
Route high-risk cases to fraud support. Route repeat contacts or urgent access concerns as priority escalations.
