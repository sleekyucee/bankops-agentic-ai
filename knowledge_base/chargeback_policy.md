# Chargeback Policy

## Purpose
Use chargeback support when a customer recognizes the merchant but disputes the outcome of a purchase.

## Eligible Scenarios
- Goods or services were not received.
- The customer was charged more than expected.
- The merchant charged the customer more than once.
- A refund was promised but not received within the expected timeline.

## Not a Chargeback
Do not treat clearly unauthorized activity as a chargeback. Unauthorized activity should follow the fraud dispute process.

## Support Action
Collect transaction details, merchant contact attempts, expected refund date, and any customer-provided evidence. Create a support ticket with issue type `chargeback`.

## Customer Message
Tell the customer the dispute has been captured and explain whether the next step is merchant follow-up, chargeback review, or fraud review.
