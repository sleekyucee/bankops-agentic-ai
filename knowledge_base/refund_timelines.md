# Refund Timelines

## Purpose
Use this article to answer customer questions about expected refund timing.

## Standard Timelines
- Card refund from merchant: usually 3 to 10 business days.
- Cancelled pending card authorization: usually 1 to 7 business days.
- Duplicate transaction correction: usually 2 to 5 business days after confirmation.
- Internal adjustment: usually visible within 1 to 2 business days after approval.

## Support Action
Check whether the transaction is pending, posted, refunded, or disputed. If the refund is within the expected timeline, explain the normal processing window.

## When to Escalate
Escalate if the customer has waited beyond the expected window, has written merchant confirmation, or reports financial harm from the delay.

## Customer Message
Keep the response specific to the transaction status and avoid promising exact posting dates.
