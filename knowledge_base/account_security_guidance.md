# Account Security Guidance

## Purpose
Use this guidance when a customer reports account access concerns, suspicious login activity, or possible credential exposure.

## Immediate Steps
- Advise the customer to change their password.
- Recommend enabling multi-factor authentication if available.
- Review recent account activity with the customer.
- Freeze affected cards if card activity may be compromised.

## Support Action
Mark the account risk level based on reported activity. Route login lockouts to account support. Route suspicious transactions to fraud support.

## Customer Message
Explain practical next steps without claiming support has directly changed passwords or settings unless the action was completed in the system.

## Escalation
Escalate urgent account access loss, repeated failed verification, or combined login and payment fraud signals.
