# Escalation SLA

## Purpose
Use this article to classify support priority and set internal response expectations.

## Priority Levels
- High: active fraud, frozen account access, urgent card compromise, or repeated high-risk contacts.
- Medium: open disputes, unresolved support tickets, refund delays beyond normal timelines, or customer complaints.
- Low: general guidance, status checks within normal timelines, and non-urgent account questions.

## Target Response
- High priority: initial human review within 1 business hour.
- Medium priority: initial human review within 1 business day.
- Low priority: response through standard support queue.

## Assignment
Route fraud and suspicious transaction escalations to fraud support. Route login and access issues to account support. Route refund and chargeback questions to billing support.

## Customer Message
Tell the customer the case priority and assigned team. Avoid quoting internal SLA language unless the workflow requires it.
